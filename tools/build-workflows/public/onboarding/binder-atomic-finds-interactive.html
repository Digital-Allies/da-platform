CSV Bulk Import (Add Multiple Products at Once)

Why use CSV?

If you have 20+ products to add, don't do it one-by-one. Use a CSV (spreadsheet) file to bulk-import them in seconds.

Step 1: Prepare Your Spreadsheet

Create a Google Sheet or Excel file with these exact columns (in the first row):

Title, Price, Category, Image URL, Description

Then add one product per row:

Title Price Category Image URL Description
Vintage Rattan Chair 250.00 Seating https://example.com/chair.jpg 1970s swivel chair, fully restored
Wicker Dresser 285.00 Storage https://example.com/dresser.jpg Handwoven wicker, honey finish, 4 drawers

💡 Important CSV Tips

  • Prices: Numbers only (250.00, not $250 or "Two fifty")
  • Image URLs: Must start with https:// and be publicly accessible
  • Categories: Keep them consistent (no mixing "Chairs" and "Seating")
  • Export as CSV: Google Sheets → File → Download → CSV

Step 2: Import into Your Collection

Create or open a collection

Go to Collections and open the one you want to import into (or create a new one)

Scroll to "Import Spreadsheet Collection (CSV)"

You'll see an upload area

Upload your CSV file

Drag and drop or click to select

Review the preview

The system shows how many products it found

Click "Execute Import"

Sit back and wait (usually 30 seconds to 2 minutes)

Success!

Your products are now in the catalog and added to the collection

SEO & How to Write Great Copy

What is SEO?

SEO = Search Engine Optimization. It's how people find you on Google (and other search engines). The platform does most of this automatically. Your job: write great descriptions that help Google (and customers) understand what you're selling.

Your Brand Voice

Write like a knowledgeable, approachable curator who genuinely cares about quality vintage design.

  • ✅ Use conversational language ("This beauty deserves a forever home")
  • ✅ Tell the story ("1970s swivel chair, fully restored...")
  • ✅ Be specific about materials (rattan, bamboo, walnut, teak)
  • ✅ Highlight authenticity ("original hardware", "hand-joinery")
  • ✅ Include dimensions and condition honestly
  • ✅ Sound like yourself (short sentences, genuine enthusiasm)
  • ❌ Don't use corporate speak ("curated inventory solutions")
  • ❌ Don't say "pre-owned" — say "handpicked" or "restored"
  • ❌ Don't hide flaws — own them honestly (adds authenticity)
  • ❌ Don't sound generic (every piece is unique)
  • ❌ Don't overuse hype words ("amazing", "revolutionary")
  • ❌ Don't use multiple exclamation points (one max)

Good Product Copy Example

Title: Vintage Wicker Waterfall Dresser | Boho Coastal Rattan Chest | Hand-Restored in Austin, TX

Description: Four-drawer dresser featuring intricate handwoven wicker details in a warm honey finish. Each drawer opens smoothly with original brass hardware. No wobble, fully functional. Dimensions: 38in W x 33in D x 38in H. Patina consistent with age; one corner shows minor water staining (see photos). Restored 2026 in Austin.

Why it works: Specific materials, honest condition, dimensions, location, authentic voice

Troubleshooting & Common Questions

I published something but it's not showing on my site

+

What to check:

  • Give it 10-30 seconds for the cache to update
  • Refresh your browser (Cmd+R on Mac, Ctrl+R on Windows)
  • Make sure you clicked "Publish" (not just "Save Draft")
  • Check that the product's "Published" toggle is ON
  • If it's a product, check "In Stock" is ON

My CSV import failed with an error

+

Common causes:

  • Image URLs are broken — Test each URL in your browser first
  • Price column has non-numbers — No $ symbols or commas (285.00 ✅, $285 ❌)
  • File encoding is wrong — Re-export as UTF-8 CSV
  • Less than 2 rows — CSV must have header + at least 1 product

My images look blurry or stretched

+

What to do:

  • Use high-quality source images (600px+ wide)
  • Avoid tiny images that get enlarged
  • For CSV: make sure Image URLs point to good quality
  • Test on your live site before publishing 5+ items

Can I schedule a post to publish later?

+

Not yet. Schedule publishing is Phase 2. For now: write in Draft mode, then click Publish when you're ready to go live.

I can't log in / forgot my password

+

No passwords needed! Each time you log in:

  1. Go to cms.digitalallies.net/admin
  2. Enter your email
  3. Check your email for a login link (click it)
  4. You're logged in

If you don't see the email, check your spam folder. If still stuck, email Anthony.
