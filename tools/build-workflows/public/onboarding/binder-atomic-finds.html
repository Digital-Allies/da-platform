Section 6

Getting Found Online

6

SEO — Search Engine Optimization

SEO is how search engines like Google and Bing find and rank your website. The higher your ranking, the more people discover your shop.

What your platform does automatically

  • Sitemap — A file that tells search engines every page on your site (at atomicfindsatx.store/sitemap.xml)
  • robots.txt — Tells search engines what to index
  • Meta tags — Automatic title and description tags on every page
  • Structured data (JSON-LD) — Machine-readable information about your business (name, address, hours, products)
  • Open Graph tags — Makes your site look great when shared on social media
  • Mobile optimization — Google ranks mobile-friendly sites higher; yours is

What you control

  • Page titles (set in Settings)
  • Product names and descriptions (set in Showroom)
  • Blog post content (set in The Press Office)
  • Image file names (descriptive names like "rattan-chair-1970s.jpg" help)

AEO — Answer Engine Optimization

A newer and increasingly important category. AEO is about getting your business recognized by AI assistants — Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews.

When someone asks "Where can I find restored mid-century furniture in Austin?" your site is structured so that AI tools can read your product descriptions, your location, and your specialty and surface Atomic Finds ATX as a relevant answer.

The best thing you can do for AEO: Keep your product descriptions detailed and specific. Instead of "Rattan chair," write "1970s rattan peacock chair, fully restored, Austin TX, available for inquiry." That specificity is what AI assistants use to match your business to real questions.
Section 7

Before You Publish

7

Run through this list before making any content go live.

For products

  • Product name is clear and specific (not just "Chair" — "1970s Rattan Peacock Chair")
  • Price is correct
  • Photo URL is working (paste it in a browser to check)
  • Selling method is set correctly (Inquiry / View Listing / etc.)
  • In Stock is checked (or unchecked if sold)
  • Featured is toggled correctly (only highlight your best few pieces)

For blog posts and content

  • Title is engaging and describes what the post is about
  • Content is proofread
  • Excerpt (short summary) is filled in
  • Any linked URLs are working

For page edits

  • Preview the change in the live preview panel before publishing
  • Check the mobile view looks correct
  • Contact info is accurate if you edited the contact section

Appendix

Glossary

A

Terms you'll encounter in your CMS and in conversations with Digital Allies:

CMS (Content Management System)
The platform you use to manage your website without coding. Your CMS is at cms.digitalallies.net/admin.
Block / Section
A reusable chunk of a webpage — like a hero banner, a product grid, a contact form, or a text section. Pages are built by stacking blocks.
Draft
Content that's saved but not visible to the public yet. Drafts are for work in progress. Set status to "Published" to make content visible.
Published
Content that's live and visible to all visitors on your site.
SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
The practice of making your site easy for Google, Bing, and other search engines to find and rank. Good SEO means more people discover your site naturally.
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
A newer practice focused on making your business visible in AI-powered search tools — Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI. These tools pull from structured content on your site.
Design Tokens
The core set of colors, fonts, and spacing values that define your brand on the platform. These live in Brand Theme and apply across every page of your site automatically.
Collection
A curated group of products. Think of it like a playlist — you pick which items go in it and give it a name. Collections appear as category tabs on your storefront.
RLS (Row Level Security)
Behind-the-scenes database protection that ensures your data is only visible to you and your customers — not other businesses on the same platform.
Supabase
The secure database that stores all your content — products, settings, pages, reviews. You interact with it through your CMS, not directly.
Vercel
The hosting service that runs your live website. It automatically updates your site whenever changes are published. You never need to log into it.
