# Atomic Finds ATX — Brand Guide & Training Templates

## Brand Story

**Who you are:** Atomic Finds is a celestial-vintage storefront in Austin, Texas, specializing in handpicked, restored mid-century and vintage furniture, home decor, and architectural salvage.

**Your mission:** "Far-out finds, down-to-earth prices" — Sourcing the most soulful vintage pieces and giving them a second life with integrity and care.

**Your customer:** People who value authenticity, sustainability, and design-forward vintage style. They appreciate the story behind each piece and are willing to invest in quality furniture.

- This creates maximum contrast and drama while keeping the site readable

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Core Identity

Atomic Finds speaks as a **knowledgeable, approachable curator**. You're not a corporate seller — you're a trusted friend who knows vintage design and genuinely cares about quality.

### Do's ✅

- **Use conversational language:** "We handpick..." not "We have procured..."
- **Tell the story:** "1970s swivel chair, fully restored, still has original tags" vs. just "chair"
- **Be specific about materials:** Always name materials (rattan, bamboo, teak, walnut)
- **Highlight authenticity:** "Original hardware," "hand-joinery," "period-correct restoration"
- **Use warm, inviting descriptions:** "This beauty deserves a forever home"
- **Sound like yourself:** Short sentences, genuine enthusiasm, real observations
- **Include dimensions and condition:** Help customers visualize the piece

### Don'ts ❌

- **Avoid corporate speak:** No "curated inventory management solutions"
- **Don't use clinical terms:** "Pre-owned" ❌ → "Handpicked" ✅, "Restored" ✅
- **Don't hide flaws:** Own up to wear, patina, repairs honestly (it adds authenticity)
- **Don't sound generic:** Every piece is unique — describe what makes THIS piece special
- **Don't use hype words:** No "Amazing," "Revolutionary," "Game-changing"
- **Avoid exclamation points in excess:** One per product description max

### Examples: Good Copy

**Product Title:**
"Vintage Wicker Waterfall Dresser | Boho Coastal Rattan Chest | Hand-Restored in Austin, TX"

**Product Description:**
"Four-drawer dresser featuring intricate handwoven wicker details in a warm honey finish. Each drawer opens smoothly with original brass hardware. No wobble, fully functional. Dimensions: 38in W x 33in D x 38in H. Patina consistent with age; one corner shows minor water staining (see photos). Restored 2026 in Austin."

**Homepage Tagline:**
"Sourced with an eye. Restored with love."

When writing product descriptions or creating collections:

1. **Assign each product to one mascot** (based on the product's primary vibe)
2. **Use their voice** when writing descriptions (Daisy = comfort, Milo = craft, Tatiana = drama, Malibu = social)
3. **Create collections by mascot** ("Daisy's Lounge", "Milo's Masterpieces", "Tatiana's Showstoppers", "Malibu's Entertaining")
4. **Visual cues** (optional): Each collection gets a light color wash or pattern accent matching the mascot's energy

## SEO & AEO Best Practices for Atomic Finds

### Material-First Naming

Every product title should **lead with material and style**, then add descriptive details:

❌ **Don't:**
- "Vintage Chair"
- "Retro Desk"
- "Old Cabinet"

✅ **Do:**
- "Vintage Rattan Peacock Chair | Boho Mid-Century Accent Seating"
- "Solid Walnut Credenza | Mid-Century Modern Sideboard"
- "Hand-Painted Ceramic Cabinet | Handmade Artisan Storage | Austin, TX"

### Geographic Anchoring

Always include local references when applicable:

- "Hand-restored in Austin, TX"
- "Available for local pickup in South Austin"
- "Austin-based vintage restoration"
- "Sourced from Central Texas estates"

Why: People searching "vintage furniture Austin" or "rattan chairs Austin" will find you.

### Condition & Care Language

Use consistent, honest terminology:

- **Excellent:** Minimal wear, fully original, pristine condition
- **Very Good:** Light wear consistent with age, fully functional
- **Good:** Visible wear, minor repairs, fully functional, character marks
- **Fair:** Heavy wear, significant repairs, quirky character, functional with care
- Always say: "See photos for condition details"

### Material-Specific Keywords

Include these in descriptions for AI search engines:

- Material: Rattan, Bamboo, Walnut, Teak, Wicker, Woven, Solid Wood
- Era: 1970s, Mid-Century, 1960s, Retro, Vintage
- Style: Boho, Coastal, Modern, Scandinavian, Eclectic
- Condition: Restored, Hand-picked, Authentic, Original, Reclaimed
- Size/Type: Dresser, Credenza, Chair, Desk, Accent Piece

### Example: AEO-Optimized Description

```
Title: Vintage Wicker Waterfall Dresser | Boho Coastal Rattan Chest | Hand-Restored in Austin, TX

Description:
Four-drawer dresser featuring intricate handwoven wicker construction with a warm honey finish. 
Authentic 1970s piece with original brass drawer pulls. Hand-restored in Austin, TX in 2026.

Materials: Wicker, Bamboo frame, Solid brass hardware
Condition: Excellent — minimal wear, fully functional
Dimensions: 38in W x 33in D x 38in H
Style: Boho, Coastal, Retro '70s
Era: 1970s
Location: Austin, TX — available for local pickup or shipping

Sourced from Central Texas estate collections. Each wicker strand still shows the artisan's hand-work. 
No wobble, drawers glide smoothly. This piece is ready for its forever home.
```

## Glossary of Atomic Finds Terms

| Term | Meaning (Atomic Finds Edition) |
|------|------|
| **Handpicked** | Personally curated from estate sales, vintage dealers, and local sources |
| **Restored** | Cleaned, repaired, and refreshed to working condition |
| **Patina** | The honest wear and aging of a piece — adds character, not detraction |
| **Era** | Decade the piece was manufactured (1970s, 1960s, etc.) |
| **Original Hardware** | The metal pulls, knobs, feet, etc. that came with the piece originally |
| **Fully Functional** | Works as intended; no active issues |
| **Local Pickup** | Customer collects from your Austin location (faster, often cheaper) |
| **Condition Marks** | Natural signs of age — scratches, wear, patina |
| **Solid Wood** | Real wood construction (not veneer or particleboard) |
| **Hand-Joinery** | Traditional woodworking joints (dovetails, mortise-and-tenon) |
