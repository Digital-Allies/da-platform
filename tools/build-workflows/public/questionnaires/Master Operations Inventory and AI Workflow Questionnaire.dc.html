2.5 Data-Quality Controls

The following rules apply to all inventory records, public content, and any system or tool that reads from or writes to the inventory.

SKU

Must be unique and exactly four digits. No duplicates permitted.

Status

Must use only the exact approved status vocabulary defined in section 2.3. No freeform or alternate labels.

Dates

All dates use YYYY-MM-DD format throughout the inventory system and any export or template.

Price

Uses a consistent currency format. Remains internal until Jennyfer has approved it for public display.

Marketplace URL

Must be item-specific before it can trigger a “View on Marketplace” CTA on the website. Generic or storefront-level URLs do not qualify.

Unknown factual fields

Must be left blank, marked unknown, or retained as an internal verification task. They may never be guessed, inferred from a photo alone, or filled by an AI without a verified source.

Public fields

Must match verified inventory data exactly. No public-facing copy may be written from memory, an old caption, or an unverified source.

Product photos & marketing assets

Must be final PNG files following the approved naming rule before they can be used in any public-facing context.
