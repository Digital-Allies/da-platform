## Research Prompt #1: Mission & Core Values

**What this produces:** Mission statement + 4-5 core values with examples, anti-values, and connections to the rattan deep dive.

**Perplexity Prompt:**

> Based on the three Atomic Finds ATX foundation documents I've shared (product taxonomy, rattan deep dive, and website copy approach), synthesize a mission statement and core values framework for the brand.
>
> Key context to embed:
> - Atomic Finds ATX is owned and run by Jennyfer Gomez, a Mexican-American curator and restorer
> - The brand philosophy centers on authentication, restoration, and cultural accountability
> - The tagline is "Far-out finds, down-to-earth prices" (80% grounded furniture expertise, 15% warmth, 5% sparkle)
> - Four playful Curators (Daisy, Milo, Tatiana, Malibu) guide customers by function/feeling, but Jennyfer is the real authority
> - The voice prioritizes authenticity, specificity, and honesty about vintage condition over hype
> - Sustainability is positioned as "radical sustainability through curation," not greenwashing
> - Austin is integral to identity (local pickup, sourcing, community, creative heritage)
>
> Deliver:
> 1. A single mission statement (1-2 sentences) that captures the soul of the business and honors Jennyfer's role
> 2. 4-5 core values (names + 1-sentence descriptions) that differentiate Atomic Finds from typical vintage furniture sellers
> 3. For each value, provide 3 concrete examples of how it shows up in daily operations (customer communication, product sourcing, curation decisions, website copy)
> 4. For each value, note what anti-values or misconceptions the brand actively rejects (e.g., "Authenticity vs. Greenwashing")
> 5. Map how each value connects back to specific sections of the Rattan Deep Dive (cultural accountability, colonial extraction awareness, BIPOC reclamation, etc.)

**Expected output:** ~2,000 words  
**Time to run:** 10-15 minutes  
**Deliverable title:** "Atomic Finds Mission & Core Values Framework"

## Research Prompt #2: Blog & Website Copy Style Guide

**What this produces:** Blog post structure, content pillars, opening hooks, website copy variations, tone expansion rules.

**Perplexity Prompt:**

> Develop a comprehensive blog, website, and long-form content style guide for Atomic Finds ATX based on the existing copy approach document I'm sharing.
>
> The existing "Full Website Copy Approach" establishes a voice rule: "80% grounded furniture expertise, 15% warmth and personality, 5% space-age sparkle." It also specifies:
> - Required words: handpicked, restored, 1970s, mid-century, rattan, bamboo, wicker, patina, built to last, ready for its forever home, Austin
> - Avoid: pre-owned, refurbished, generic luxury language, overblown claims, fake scarcity, long cosmic metaphors
> - Every page should: (1) help picture the piece at home, (2) prove why it's special, (3) make vintage feel approachable, (4) give a clear next step
>
> Now extend these principles to blog/journal content with:
>
> 1. Blog voice & tone framework
>    - How to maintain the 80/15/5 formula in 600-2000 word posts
>    - Jennyfer's voice vs. brand voice (when to use "I" for founder, "we" for brand)
>    - How to be specific and opinionated without being salesy
>    - How the Four Curators fit into blog content
>
> 2. Blog content pillars & angles
>    - Design History (era/material context, movements, artistry)
>    - Sourcing Stories (Jennyfer's hunts, how pieces are found, decision-making)
>    - Cultural & Material Context (tie to rattan deep dive: colonial history, artisan roots, Austin community)
>    - Restoration & Care (how pieces are restored, maintenance tips, longevity)
>    - Styling & Inspiration (how customers can use pieces, mood boards, room concepts)
>    - Sustainability & Values (radical vintage, BIPOC curation, anti-greenwashing)
>    - Behind-the-Scenes (workshop, decisions, process, personality)
>
> 3. Blog post structure & formulas
>    - Opening hooks for each content pillar (5+ examples)
>    - Middle structure: balance of narrative, specificity, and takeaway
>    - How to weave in SEO keywords naturally (from product taxonomy keyword bank)
>    - How to close with action (link to piece, invite questions, encourage discovery)
>
> 4. Website copy template expansion
>    - Homepage hero variations for seasonal/thematic rotations
>    - About page depth: telling Jennyfer's story without overshadowing the work
>    - Collection page copy that reinforces both category function AND curatorial personality
>    - Journal/blog index page that sets expectations for content quality
>
> 5. Specific deliverables
>    - 5 complete blog post examples (600-1000 words each), showing all 5 content pillars
>    - Homepage hero variations (3-4 alternatives to rotate seasonally)
>    - About page long-form (800-1000 words, balancing Jennyfer's personal story with mission)
>    - A one-page quick-reference for all writers/contributors covering tone, word choices, structure
>    - A content calendar template showing how to distribute topics across 12 weeks

**Expected output:** ~4,000 words  
**Time to run:** 15-20 minutes  
**Deliverable title:** "Atomic Finds Blog & Website Copy Master Guide"

## Research Prompt #3: Social Media & Micro-Content Style Guide

**What this produces:** Platform-specific captions, content pillar examples, posting frequency, hashtag strategy, visual language.

**Perplexity Prompt:**

> Develop a social media content style guide for Atomic Finds ATX across Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, and TikTok. Apply the same voice rules (80% expertise, 15% warmth, 5% sparkle) but compressed for social media's fast-scrolling environment.
>
> Provide:
>
> 1. Platform-specific strategies
>    - Instagram: visual-first, 150-300 word captions; curation + storytelling + engagement; Reels for restoration/process
>    - Facebook: longer narratives (300-600 words), community building, customer testimonials, link to journal posts
>    - Pinterest: SEO-optimized pin descriptions, link back to blog/product pages, seasonal themes
>    - TikTok: 30-60 second format, Jennyfer as talent, behind-the-scenes restoration, quick curation tips, personality-forward
>
> 2. Caption formulas for each pillar
>    - Product launches (5-7 variations showing different angles: material story, silhouette, condition, mood, fit)
>    - Find backstory (the hunt, why it's special, specifics about restoration or condition)
>    - Styling prompts (mood boards, use cases, room pairings, who this is for)
>    - Educational tips (quick care advice, spotting quality, material knowledge)
>    - Sustainability angle (extending life, avoiding waste, honoring original artisans)
>    - Behind-the-scenes (restoration process, sourcing stories, Jennyfer's decision-making)
>    - Customer stories (tagged, with permission; real homes using Atomic Finds pieces)
>
> 3. Content pillars & posting frequency
>    - Curation / New Finds (2-3x per week)
>    - Restoration / Education (1x per week)
>    - Styling Inspiration (1x per week)
>    - Behind-the-Scenes (2x per month)
>    - Customer Features (2x per month)
>    - Engagement / Questions (1x per week)
>
> 4. Visual & hashtag strategy
>    - Color palette consistency (dark backgrounds, celestial yellow highlights, natural rattan tones)
>    - Photography: styled but lived-in, moody lighting, honest condition showing
>    - Text overlays: keep minimal, use brand fonts (DM Sans for body, Bagel Fat One for emphasis)
>    - Branded hashtags to build community (e.g., #AtomicFindsStory, #FarOutFind, #RestoredWithCare)
>    - Research hashtags by pillar (curation, vintage furniture, rattan, MCM, Austin design, sustainability)
>
> 5. Deliverables
>    - 12-15 complete caption examples covering all pillars and platforms
>    - Platform-specific quick-reference sheets (what works on Instagram vs. TikTok)
>    - 12-week social calendar template showing posting frequency and pillar distribution
>    - Hashtag bank (25-30 researched tags per pillar)
>    - Bio copy variations for each platform

**Expected output:** ~3,500 words  
**Time to run:** 15-20 minutes  
**Deliverable title:** "Atomic Finds Social Media Master Guide"

## Research Prompt #4: Bilingual & Spanish Content Strategy

**What this produces:** Spanish voice guidelines, culturally-rooted content approaches, bilingual architecture, Mexican/Mexican-American context.

**Perplexity Prompt:**

> Develop bilingual content guidelines for Atomic Finds ATX with a focus on authentic Spanish-language content (not machine translation) for audiences in Mexico City and Austin's Mexican-American community.
>
> Critical context:
> - Atomic Finds is Mexican-American-owned by Jennyfer Gomez
> - The brand's Rattan Deep Dive emphasizes cultural accountability and honoring global artisans
> - Spanish content should NOT be word-for-word translations of English copy
> - Instead, Spanish content should speak directly to the cultural heritage of woven furniture (mimbre, palma traditions in Mexico)
> - The audience includes both Spanish-fluent collectors in Mexico and heritage/bilingual audiences in Austin
>
> Provide:
>
> 1. Spanish voice & tone
>    - How to adapt the 80/15/5 formula for Spanish speakers
>    - Required Spanish vocabulary (material names: mimbre, palma, junco, rattan, bambú; quality descriptors: restauración, artesanal, auténtico)
>    - Tone: formal enough to convey expertise, warm enough to feel like Jennyfer speaking directly
>    - Use of "tú" vs. "usted" (recommend: tú for social/blog to feel personal; usted for formal product descriptions)
>
> 2. Mexican cultural context for woven furniture
>    - Historical connection between Mexican artisan traditions and vintage rattan/bamboo
>    - How to reference mimbre and palma traditions without cultural appropriation
>    - Tie to artisan communities in Mexico that influenced mid-century design
>    - Language that honors Jennyfer's identity as a curator connecting two cultures
>
> 3. Spanish product descriptions
>    - Product title formula in Spanish (similar to English, but with cultural awareness)
>    - Description framework adapted for Spanish speakers
>    - How to describe condition/patina for Spanish audience
>    - Examples: 5 complete product descriptions in Spanish
>
> 4. Spanish blog & website copy
>    - Homepage hero in Spanish (versions for Mexico City vs. Austin bilingual audience)
>    - About Jennyfer in Spanish (emphasizing her Mexican-American identity and curatorial mission)
>    - Blog post examples (3 showing design history, cultural context, restoration)
>    - Social media captions in Spanish
>
> 5. Bilingual website architecture
>    - How to signal language choice without making it feel like translation
>    - Keeping Jennyfer's voice consistent across both languages
>    - SEO keyword strategy for Spanish search
>    - Spanish hashtag bank for social media
>
> 6. Deliverables
>    - Full Spanish website copy (homepage, about, collections, how it works, contact)
>    - 5 blog post examples in Spanish
>    - 10-12 social media captions in Spanish
>    - Spanish product description template + 5 complete examples
>    - Language strategy guide: when to translate, when to write fresh
>    - Spanish SEO keyword bank (40-50 search terms)
>    - Bilingual voice consistency checklist

**Expected output:** ~3,000 words  
**Time to run:** 15-20 minutes  
**Deliverable title:** "Atomic Finds Spanish & Bilingual Content Master Guide"

## Research Prompt #5: Product Description Style

**What this produces:** Product description templates, specific material language, condition/restoration messaging, examples.

**Perplexity Prompt:**

> Create a product description framework for Atomic Finds that balances storytelling with specificity. Each product listing should sell the piece, not the transaction.
>
> Provide:
>
> 1. Description structure
>    - Hook (1 sentence that captures the mood/vibe)
>    - Story (era, style, estimated origin, any historical context)
>    - Technical specs (materials, dimensions, condition, what's been restored)
>    - The ask (who this is for, how to use it, where it belongs)
>    - Honesty (flaws, patina, what makes it real)
>
> 2. Writing formulas
>    - How to describe rattan/wicker pieces (texture, construction)
>    - How to write about MCM furniture (lines, materials, designer influence if known)
>    - How to describe condition honestly without dampening appeal
>    - How to make dimensions matter (not just "W x D x H")
>    - How to use patina language appropriately (Spanish/English)
>
> 3. Examples
>    - 5 complete product descriptions (400-500 words each) showing range:
>      - Rattan lounge chair (condition-forward, storytelling)
>      - Wicker dresser (materials + craftsmanship focus)
>      - Bamboo bar cart (visual/functional appeal)
>      - MCM dining table (era + silhouette emphasis)
>      - Plant stand or small find (personality in brevity)
>    - How to handle common concerns (shipping, condition questions, styling)
>
> 4. Deliverables
>    - Template/formula that any team member can follow
>    - Examples showing different product categories
>    - FAQ response templates for common customer questions

**Expected output:** ~2,500 words  
**Time to run:** 10-15 minutes  
**Deliverable title:** "Atomic Finds Product Description Master Guide"

## Assembly Instructions

Once all 5 Perplexity research outputs are complete:

1. **Compile into master document** — Combine all 5 deliverables into one Google Doc or Markdown file titled "Atomic Finds Brand Voice Master Guide"

2. **Create quick-reference sheets** — Extract the most-used formulas and checklists into separate one-page PDFs for day-to-day use:
   - Social media caption quick-ref
   - Blog post structure quick-ref
   - Product description template
   - Voice checklist
   - Spanish translation guidelines

3. **Train the team** — Share master guide with Jennyfer, Anthony, and any content creators/social team members. Schedule a 1-hour walkthrough.

4. **Test & refine** — Use the guidelines to produce 4 weeks of social content, 3 blog posts, and 10 new product descriptions. Review after 30 days and refine based on performance and feedback.

5. **Archive & maintain** — Store the master guide in a shared folder (Notion, Google Drive, or in the CMS as a reference doc). Update quarterly as the brand voice evolves.

By the end of 4 weeks of using these guidelines, measure:
